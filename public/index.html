FAQ

Questions clients ask.

How much does a website cost?

Sites start at $500, and every project gets a free quote based on what your site needs. You can start with a simple site and add an admin panel, online payments or automatic emails later, so you only pay for what you use.

How long does it take?

About 1–2 weeks for a simple site, and about 3–6 weeks if it includes an admin panel or online payments.

Who owns the site?

You own your domain and everything on your site: your text, photos and customer data. The site keeps running as long as you're on hosting & maintenance. If you ever want to move it somewhere else, we'll work out a handover together.

What does hosting & maintenance cover?

I host your site, keep it updated and secure, and make small changes for you, all for one monthly fee. I make sure hosting stays affordable.

Can I update the site myself?

With an admin panel, yes: you can edit text, events, products and more without calling me. Without one, just send me what you want changed.

Do I need a domain already?

No. If you have one, we'll use it. If not, I'll help you pick one and set it up in your name.
